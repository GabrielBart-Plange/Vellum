# Vellum - Monetization & Gamification Strategy (Formalized)

This document outlines the core monetization and gamification framework for Vellum, combining high-revenue performance with community-centric growth.

## 1. The Three-Pillar Currency System
*   **Gilt (Hard)**: The "Gold Leaf" currency. Purchased with real money (MoMo/Card).
    *   **Value**: **10 Gilt = GHS 2.00** (1 Gilt ≈ $0.018 USD | 100 Gilt ≈ $1.85 USD).
    *   **Exchange Ratio**: **1 Gilt = 10 Inklets** (Purchasing power equivalent).
    *   **Usage**: Permanent chapter unlocks, tipping authors, purchasing exclusive digital items.
    *   **Author Split**: 70% of Gilt spent goes to the creator.
*   **Inklets (Soft/Rewards)**: Daily reward currency. Earned through engagement (check-ins, missions, ads).
    *   **Value**: **100 Inklets = GHS 2.00** (Aligned with Gilt: 10 Gilt = 100 Inklets).
    *   **Usage**: Temporary/Standard chapter unlocks (FastPass style). Cannot be used for tipping.
*   **Vellux (Support Tokens)**: Tiered ranking tokens (**Gold, Diamond, Platinum**).
    *   **Usage**: Influence "Archival Rankings" and boost story visibility.
    *   **Acquisition**: Granted to high-tier subscribers and milestone Gilt spenders.

## 2. Subscription Tiers
*   **Vellum Plus (GHS 10/week | ~$0.98 USD/week)**:
    *   Ad-Free reading experience.
    *   24h Early Access to new chapters.
    *   Monthly Bonus: 50 Inklets.
    *   Referral Reward Limit: Up to **25 successful inductions**.
*   **Vellum Pro (GHS 30/month | ~$2.78 USD/month)**:
    *   **All Plus Perks**: Includes ad-free and early access.
    *   **The Pro Pool**: Access to "Premium Picks" (3 free Gilt-locked chapters per month).
    *   **Vellux Grant**: 1 Gold Vellux per month.
    *   Exclusive Profile Aura & Badge.
    *   Referral Reward Limit: Up to **50 successful inductions**.

## 3. Revenue Sharing (The "Archivist's Cut")
*   **Authors**: 70% of Gilt tips and chapter unlocks.
*   **Ad Revenue**: 70% share for authors exceeding viewership thresholds.
*   **Platform**: 25% for maintenance, server costs, and marketing.
*   **Level 9 Pool**: 5% distributed to the top 1% of contributors (The Elders).

## 4. Gamification & Level 9 Mechanics
*   **XP Progression**: Earned via reading, commenting, and community interaction.
*   **Level 9 (The Sovereign)**:
    *   **Requirement**: Max Level + "Ritual of Ascension" (Gilt contribution).
    *   **Benefit**: Passive income from the 5% pool, enhanced curation power, and elite cosmetic status.

## 5. Viral Growth: The Archivist's Echo
*   **Referral Bonus**: Refer a new chronicler to earn **50 Inklets** once they sign up.
*   **Tiered Scaling**: To maintain economic balance, reward limits are capped based on membership:
    *   **Free Users**: Limit of 10 rewards.
    *   **Plus Users**: Limit of 25 rewards.
    *   **Pro Users**: Limit of 50 rewards.

## 6. Regional Payment Routing
*   **West Africa**: Optimized for **Paystack** (Mobile Money focus).
*   **International**: Optimized for **Stripe** (USD focus).
